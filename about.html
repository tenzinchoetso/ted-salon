The founder

Ted Kunchok

Tibetan creator, 1.38 million subscribers on YouTube — and the reason Ted Salon exists. He built his channel on helping strangers, and opened the salon on the same idea.

  • 1.38Msubscribers on YouTube
  • 310videos on his channel
  • 50%of Ted Salon's profit pledged to people in need

On YouTube

Helping strangers

Ted started his channel in 2015 and became known during the 2020 lockdown for videos of him helping strangers: food for families, school fees for street kids and makeovers for people starting over.

His videos since include meal drives on the streets of Delhi, a 13-year-old boy's “magical transformation” and ₹10 lakh donated to the victims of a fire. In 2022 he became the first Tibetan creator to pass one million subscribers.

The promise

Half of the profit goes to people in need

When Ted opened the salon in 2024, he made one promise to his audience: half of all the profit goes to charitable foundations and to people in need. Every appointment is part of that.

Behind the scenes

Building the salon

Before opening day, Ted took his audience round the fourth floor of Tibet Plaza while the salon was still being built. When it opened, elders from the community cut the ribbon.

Opening day

“Love and compassion are necessities, not luxuries.”

— His Holiness the 14th Dalai Lama, the line on Ted's channel

On opening day, elders from Majnu ka Tilla greeted Ted with khatas, the silk scarves offered as a blessing.

Hair donation

Grow it. Cut it. Give it.

Sonam was nine when she came in to donate 13 inches of her hair to support cancer patients. Ted donated his own hair too. Tell us you're donating when you book, and your stylist will cut it so it's ready to give.
