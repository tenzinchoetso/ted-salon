(01) About Ted Salon

A unisex salon in the heart of Little Tibet — where every cut, colour and manicure also helps someone in need.

Ted Salon opened in 2024 on the 4th floor of Tibet Plaza, right by the main gate of Majnu ka Tilla. It's the brick-and-mortar project of Tibetan creator Ted Kunchok, who has pledged half of the salon's profit to charities and people who need it. Inside: blush walls, arched windows, gold-mirror details and a team that guests keep naming in their reviews.

(06) The founder

Built by Ted Kunchok — on compassion first.

Ted started his channel in 2015 and became known during the 2020 lockdown for videos of him helping strangers: food for families, school fees for street kids and makeovers for people starting over. In 2022 he became the first Tibetan creator to pass one million subscribers.

In 2024 he opened Ted Salon with one promise to his audience: half of all the profit goes to charitable foundations and to people in need. Every appointment is part of that.

“Love and compassion are necessities, not luxuries.”

— His Holiness the 14th Dalai Lama, the line on Ted's channel
