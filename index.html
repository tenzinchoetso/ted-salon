(01) About Ted Salon

A unisex salon in the heart of Little Tibet — where every cut, colour and manicure also helps someone in need.

Opened in 2024 by Tibetan creator Ted Kunchok on the 4th floor of Tibet Plaza, right by the main gate of Majnu ka Tilla. Half of the salon's profit is pledged to charities and people in need.
